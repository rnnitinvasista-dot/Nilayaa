How We Work

Four steps to your dream space

01
3D Spatial Scan

Laser measurement, digital twin modeling, and analysis of daylight, circulation, and acoustic parameters of your space.

02
Material Curation

Hand-selection of Italian marble slabs, veneer grains, hardware specs, and custom luminaire palettes at our Basavanagudi studio.

03
Precision Build

Off-site CNC factory fabrication with sub-millimeter precision, followed by zero-dust, managed on-site fitout.

04
White Glove Handover

Deep clean, air sanitization, smart home onboarding, and our 10-year structural warranty certificate issued on day one.
